Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

This policy covers everything Phoenix Labs makes: Phoenix Upscaler, Cloud Restore, Phoenix Motion (online and desktop), Phoenix Editor and Phoenix Local AI. It sits alongside our Terms.

Never allowed

  • Sexual, nude or sexually suggestive content of any kind.
  • Anything that sexualises, exploits or harms a minor.
  • Deepfakes: face swaps, putting a real person into a scene they weren’t in, or making someone appear to say or do something they didn’t, without their consent.
  • Content meant to deceive people about real events, to impersonate someone, or to harass, threaten or bully.
  • Violent extremism, content that promotes self-harm, or anything illegal where you or the people shown live.
  • Material you don’t have the right to use, such as someone else’s footage or artwork.

How we enforce it

Prompts for generated video are checked before anything is made, and prompts or images that ask for the content above are refused and cost nothing. Cloud jobs go through the same check. We may pause or close an account or balance that keeps asking for refused content, and we report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

AI-generated content

Phoenix Motion clips are made by AI and are marked as AI-generated in their file metadata. If you post them, follow the platform’s rules for labelling AI content. Phoenix Labs is an independent company: the open models our tools run on are integrations, and we are not affiliated with or endorsed by their makers.

Reporting

To report content made with our tools, email support@phoenixlabs.space. In Phoenix Local AI you can also use Report reply on any answer.
