## One image, one sentence

Each of these started as a single still and the prompt under it. No editing afterwards, and each is the first take. The sound came out of the same pass as the picture.

“The phoenix beats its wings and bursts into flame. Fire ripples along its feathers and glowing embers drift upward into the darkness. Static camera. The roar and crackle of fire.”

“Slow cinematic push-in toward the house. The tree's branches sway in the wind and clouds drift across the sky. Wind through dry grass.”

## Questions

### How long does a clip take?

Usually well under a minute. The first clip after a quiet spell takes a minute or two longer while a GPU loads the model.

### How long are the clips?

Five seconds at 24 frames per second, with sound made in the same pass as the picture. Tick "Make it a loop" and the clip ends where it starts, ready to repeat forever.

### Can I use the clips commercially?

Yes, within the rules on this page. Phoenix Motion runs LTX-2.5, an open video model from Lightricks, whose licence claims no rights in what you make with it.

### Do you keep my images?

No. Your image is deleted as soon as the clip is made, and finished clips are deleted after two days. Download the ones you want to keep.

### What if I don't like a clip?

Make another take with a sharper prompt. A clip that fails or gets refused costs nothing, but a clip that was made used real GPU time, so we can't refund it because it wasn't the one you pictured.

### What's a wallet key?

A private code that stands for your balance, so there's no account or password. Your browser keeps it, and your top-up receipt links back to it. Anyone with the key can spend the balance, so don't share it.

### Can an AI assistant make clips for me?

Yes. Add our MCP server to your assistant and approve it once with a spending limit (or make it an agent key in the app, under your balance), and it can make clips from your balance up to that limit. [Setup for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and others](https://phoenixlabs.space/developers).
