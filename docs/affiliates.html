Who it fits

People whose audience has old footage they care about:

  • Family history and genealogy channels
  • VHS, camcorder and retro tech channels
  • Video editing tutorials and software reviews
  • Forums and newsletters about digitising tapes and archives

The biggest group of buyers isn't tape collectors. It's people with blurry phone videos from the 2010s of kids, weddings and holidays. If you can show that, you'll do well.

What to say about it

  • $129 once, no subscription. Topaz Video costs $299 a year.
  • It runs on your own PC. Videos never leave the computer. It works on NVIDIA and AMD graphics cards.
  • The free download restores 60-second clips (with a small watermark), so people can check it on their own footage first.
  • Old VHS, camcorder, DVD and phone video. It cleans up noise, fixes interlacing and upscales to 1080p.
