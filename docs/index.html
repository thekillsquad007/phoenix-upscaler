Questions

The things people ask first

What does Phoenix Labs make?

Four video tools. Phoenix Upscaler restores and upscales old video on your own PC, for $129 once. Cloud Restore does the same restoration on our GPUs, paid per clip from $2.99. Phoenix Motion turns a still image into a five-second clip with sound, from $0.59 a clip. Phoenix Editor cuts long streams and recordings down and makes captioned Shorts, $99 with a year of updates.

Is anything a subscription?

No. The Upscaler is $129 once, with every update included. Editor is $99 with a year of updates and keeps working after that year; renewing at $49 a year only brings new features. Cloud Restore and Motion are paid per clip in dollars, with no credits and no monthly plan.

Upscaler or Cloud Restore: which one do I need?

If you have a Windows or Linux PC with a decent graphics card and more than a few clips, the $129 desktop app pays for itself quickly and nothing leaves your computer. On a Mac, a laptop or a phone, or for one or two clips, upload them to Cloud Restore and pay per clip.

What kind of old video can Phoenix fix?

Anything that looked fine on the screen it was made for and doesn't now: VHS, Hi8 and MiniDV tapes, DVDs, 8mm film scans, phone clips from the 2010s, old uploads, gameplay and webcam recordings. It removes noise, untangles interlaced motion and rebuilds detail lost to compression. It can't recover detail the camera never recorded.

Does my footage get uploaded?

Not with the desktop apps: the Upscaler and Editor do all their work on your own computer. Cloud Restore and Motion upload your file to process it and delete it automatically afterwards. Nothing you upload is used to train a model.

Can an AI assistant use Phoenix for me?

Yes. Cloud Restore and Phoenix Motion work with AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor, through our MCP server and API. Add Phoenix to the assistant, approve it once with a spending limit, and it pays from your balance, never past that limit. How to set it up.
