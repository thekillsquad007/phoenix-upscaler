FAQ

Do I buy credits?

No. You see a dollar amount for that photo or video, then pay that amount. That’s it.

Why do I need an account?

So we can issue and cancel Pro licenses properly, show receipts, let you sign out devices, and keep your job history in one place.

Are my files private?

Desktop jobs never leave your computer. Studio jobs are uploaded for processing, stored only as long as needed for download, then removed. We’ll show retention clearly at upload.

What if a job fails?

You are not charged for failed processing. Support can also refund from your account history.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes — from Account in Studio or your original checkout provider. Cancellation stops renewal; local Pro access ends at the period boundary.
