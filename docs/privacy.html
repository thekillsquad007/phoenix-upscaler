Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 July 2026. Phoenix Upscaler and Phoenix Cloud Studio are products of Phoenix Labs (“we”, “us”).

The short version

The desktop app processes your video entirely on your own computer — your footage never reaches us. The cloud studio only receives a file when you choose to upload one, uses it to run that single job, and deletes it shortly afterwards. We don’t sell your data.

Desktop app

Restoration runs locally; your video files are never uploaded. To activate a licence, the app sends your licence key and a device identifier to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to confirm the purchase and count activations. It does not send your files, their contents, or their filenames.

Cloud studio

  • Your email — collected so we can attach a receipt to the job and let you return to it. We do not send marketing email.
  • Your uploaded file — stored only to run the restore and to let you download the result, then deleted automatically (typically within 48 hours). We do not view, share, or use it to train models.
  • Payment details — entered directly with Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. We never see or store your card details.

Payments

Purchases (the desktop licence and each cloud job) are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the Merchant of Record and handles billing, tax, and card data under its own privacy policy.

Analytics

We may use privacy-preserving, cookie-free website analytics (aggregate page counts only) to understand traffic. This does not identify you or track you across other sites.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any cloud job data we still hold, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing support@phoenixlabs.space. Because uploads are deleted automatically, there is usually nothing left to remove.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@phoenixlabs.space.
